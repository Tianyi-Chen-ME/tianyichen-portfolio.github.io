About Me

Hello, I'm Troy!

I'm originally from Nanjing, growing up just a few minutes from the city. Growing up, I found myself intrigued by how everything works and operates around me, from cars, to planes, to gear shifters on bikes, all of which led me to study and pursue a career in mechanical engineering, where I get to learn more deeply on how machines or parts work together to achieve a defined task.

Outside of engineering, some of my hobbies include fencing, badminton, and hanging out with friends. I also enjoy nature, going out for walks, camping, trying different types of foods.
